# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Everyone who takes part in this project, whether by writing code, reporting a problem,
reviewing, or asking a question, should be able to do so without harassment and without
being made to feel unwelcome, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Give feedback that is specific and that the other person can act on. Accept it in the
  same spirit.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was one.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, say so, and fix them.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, and demeaning or discriminatory remarks.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Where this applies

In every space the project uses, including the issue tracker, pull requests and review
comments, and anywhere a person is representing the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, contact the project maintainers
privately. Use the private reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and
say that the report is about conduct. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will
review each report, and will tell you what they decide where they can.

## What maintainers will do

Maintainers may remove or edit comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may warn, temporarily exclude or permanently exclude a person from the
project, in proportion to what happened. A maintainer who is the subject of a report takes
no part in handling it.
